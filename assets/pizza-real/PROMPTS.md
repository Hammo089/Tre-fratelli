# Photographic Neapolitan pizza assets

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, with transparent_background=true. The original PNG outputs remain untouched; the project uses alpha-preserving WebP encodings for web delivery.

Shared brief: photoreal food studio photography, perfectly overhead at 90 degrees, soft neutral diffuse overhead lighting, transparent background, no plate/table/board/text/illustration/CGI. Each ingredient is a separate full-canvas layer with separated pieces. CSS crops individual cutouts from these unchanged layers and places them naturally on the base.

- dough: one bare wood-fired Neapolitan base, airy puffed cornicione, golden leopard char spots, thin pale center, no sauce/cheese/toppings.
- sauce: one centered circular spread of crushed rich red San Marzano tomatoes, visible natural pulp and slight olive oil gloss.
- cheese: irregular softened fior di latte chunks, creamy ivory, tiny toasted golden spots, natural glossy edges.
- pepperoni: individual cooked round slices, deep red with fat flecks and crisp curled edges.
- mushroom: thin roasted button mushroom cross-sections, beige flesh, brown gills.
- olive: thin black olive rings with transparent central holes.
- pepper: curved roasted green bell pepper strips.
- onion: thin red onion rings/half-rings, pale translucent pink-white flesh.
- basil: separate fresh vivid green basil leaves with natural veins.
- tomato: thin ripe fresh red tomato slices, juicy pulp and translucent seeds.
- meat: ultra-thin cured bresaola slices, burgundy, softly folded edges.
- turkey: thin smoked turkey breast slices, pale pink-ivory, lightly browned edges.

Requested arrangement for ingredient prompts: scattered evenly inside a centered circle approximately 70% of square canvas diameter, open gaps between pieces, generous transparent outer margin. Source atlases were inspected and component boundaries recorded in pieces.js; the renderer uses them at ingredient-specific sizes.
